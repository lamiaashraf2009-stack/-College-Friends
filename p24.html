💌 To My Three Crazy Friends

Dear my three favourite people, 💗

Tora amar college life-er ekdom notun chapter-er prothom kichu beautiful memory. Hoyto amader friendship ekhono notun, ekhono amra eke oporke purota chini na, but amar mone hoy amader aro onek golpo, onek hasi, onek paglamo ar onek memory ekhono baki ache. 🌷 Ami chai, ajker ei notun friendship ta ekdin emon ekta friendship hok, jar kotha mone porle amader mukhe automatically ekta smile chole ashbe. Class-er boring time, break-er adda, ekshathe ghura, random photo, faltu kotha niye hasahasi—shob kichui ekdin memory hoye jabe. 🫶 Ar ha, majhe majhe amader moddhe misunderstanding hote pare, rag-abiman hote pare, kintu ami chai kono choto bishoy jeno amader friendship-ke noshto na kore. Karon kichu manush notun hoyeo khub taratari nijer moto hoye jay—tora amar jonno temoni kichu manush. ❤️ College life koto diner, amra keu jani na. Kintu jotodin amra ekshathe achi, totodin jeno mon khule enjoy kori, hashi, paglamo kori ar emon kichu memory banai ja future-e boshe boshe mone kore bolte pari— “Dhur, college life ta ashole onek shundor chilo!” 🥹✨ Thank you tora amar college-er notun journey-ta eto beautiful kore tulish. Ami genuinely happy je amar ei journey-te tora achish. 🤍 New friends today, unforgettable memories tomorrow. 🌸 With lots of love, Tor friend, Lamia ♡
